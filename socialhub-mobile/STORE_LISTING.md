## Full description

SocialHub is a fast, friendly social network that keeps you connected with the people who matter.

**What you can do**

- **Home feed** — browse posts from you and your friends with smooth infinite scrolling and pull-to-refresh.
- **Posts & media** — share text, photos and videos, like and react, comment, share and save.
- **Stories** — post photo and video stories that disappear after 24 hours, with an easy viewer.
- **Profiles** — a rich profile with bio, cover photo, friends, posts, and full edit tools.
- **Friends** — send and accept friend requests, see who's online, and manage your friend list.
- **Search** — find people by name or username instantly.
- **Notifications** — real-time alerts for friend requests, likes and comments, with unread badges.
- **Messaging** — real-time private chat with read receipts, typing indicators, online presence, photo messages, and quoted replies.
- **Dark mode** — light, dark and system themes.

**Privacy & security**

Your private messages stay between you and the recipient. Row-level security protects your data, and you control your information. You can delete your account at any time from Settings, which permanently removes your data.
