## Privacy Policy (SocialHub 1.0.0)

**Effective date: 19 August 2026**

### 1. Overview
SocialHub is a social networking application ("the App" or "SocialHub") that lets people share posts, stories, photos and messages, and connect with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what information SocialHub collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using SocialHub, you agree to this policy.

### 2. Information we collect
- **Account information:** email address, full name, and username provided at signup.
- **Profile information:** information you add to your profile (bio, location, work, education, website, birthday, profile photo, cover photo).
- **Content you create:** posts, comments, likes, shares, stories, and messages. Messages are private between sender and recipient.
- **Interactions:** friendships, friend requests, saved posts, notification preferences.
- **Device information:** a push-notification token and basic platform information (e.g. Android).
- **Technical data:** connection status and sanitized diagnostic error logs (no private content).

### 3. How we use information
- Provide, operate and maintain the App.
- Allow posting, friends, real-time chat and notifications.
- Send opted-in push notifications (messages, friend requests, activity).
- Keep the App secure, investigate misuse, improve performance.
- Respond to requests and provide support.

### 4. How we share information
SocialHub does not sell personal information.
- **With other users:** published content is visible per each feature's privacy settings; private messages are visible only to sender and recipient.
- **With service providers:** Supabase (database, auth, storage, realtime) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging / Expo push service for notifications.
- **Legal requirements:** where required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property.

### 5. Data storage and security
Data is stored on Supabase's secure cloud servers. HTTPS, row-level security, encrypted device session storage. Passwords are never stored by the App; authentication is handled by Supabase Auth.

### 6. Account deletion
Settings → Delete account permanently removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, stories, friendships, messages and all associated data. Not reversible. Alternative: email support for manual processing.

### 7. Your choices
Notifications on/off (device settings or permission prompt); edit/delete your own content; log out on any device.

### 8. Children's privacy
Not directed at children under 13; no knowing collection of children's data; deletion on request.

### 9. Changes to this policy
Effective date is updated when changed; continued use constitutes acceptance.
