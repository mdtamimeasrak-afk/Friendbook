# SocialHub 1.0.0 — Release Notes

- Social feed with infinite scroll and pull-to-refresh
- Posts: text, photos and videos, with like, comment, share and save
- Stories: photo and video stories that expire after 24 hours
- Profiles: bio, cover photo, friends, posts, and full profile editing
- Friends: friend requests, accept/cancel/decline, online status
- Search: find people by name or username
- Notifications: real-time alerts with unread badges
- Messaging: real-time private chat with read receipts, typing indicators,
  online presence, photo messages, quoted replies and message retry
- Dark mode: light, dark and system themes
- Push notifications (messages, friend requests, activity) on supported devices
- Security: row-level database security, encrypted sessions, no ads,
  account deletion from Settings
- Performance and stability improvements
