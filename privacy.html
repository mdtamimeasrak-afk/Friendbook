SocialHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026

1. Overview

SocialHub is a social networking application ("the App" or "SocialHub") that lets people share posts, stories, photos and messages, and connect with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what information SocialHub collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using SocialHub, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, full name, and username that you provide when you sign up.
  • Profile information: information you choose to add to your profile, such as a bio, location, work, education, website, birthday, profile photo and cover photo.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, likes, shares, stories, and messages you send. Messages are private between the sender and the recipient.
  • Interactions: friendships, friend requests, saved posts, and notification preferences.
  • Device information: a push-notification token used to deliver notifications to your device, and basic platform information (for example, that you use the Android version).
  • Technical data: connection status and diagnostic error logs that do not contain your private content.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the App and its features.
  • To let you post content, follow friends, chat in real time and receive notifications.
  • To send push notifications you have opted in to (messages, friend requests, and activity updates).
  • To keep the App secure, investigate misuse, and improve performance and reliability.
  • To respond to your requests and provide support.

4. How we share information

SocialHub does not sell your personal information. We share information only as described here:

  • With other users: content you choose to publish (posts, stories, comments, profile information) is visible to other users according to the privacy settings of each feature. Private messages are only visible to the sender and recipient.
  • With service providers: SocialHub is built on Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and realtime services) and uses Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging / Expo's push service to deliver notifications. These providers process data to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of SocialHub, its users, or the public.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers operated by Supabase. We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), row-level security on the database, and encrypted storage for your login session on your device. Your password is never stored by the App; authentication is handled by Supabase Auth.

6. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the App: Settings → Delete account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, stories, friendships, messages and all associated data from the App's systems. This action cannot be undone. If you cannot delete your account through the App, contact us using the details below and we will process your request.

7. Your choices

  • Notifications: you can enable or disable push notifications from your device settings, or decline the permission prompt.
  • Profile and content: you can edit or delete your profile information and your own posts, stories, comments and messages.
  • Log out: you can log out of your account on any device.

8. Children's privacy

SocialHub is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of SocialHub after changes means you accept the updated policy.
